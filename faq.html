Everything about Bobba bubble tea

From the flavours offered through the nutritional values and calories to the manufacture of the juice pearls by our exclusive partner in Taiwan, we tell you everything about Bobba!

HOW IS BOBBABUBBLE TEA MADE?

Making Bobba ready-to-drink bubble tea is serious business!

We start by crafting our unique juice pearls in Taiwan, using only the finest ingredients. These pearls are then carefully blended with our delicious tea and natural flavors to create the perfect balance of taste and texture. Each bottle is crafted with care to ensure that every sip delivers the authentic bubble tea experience you love.

WHAT MAKES BOBBA UNIQUE?

Bobba is the very first ready-to-drink bubble tea to make its debut in supermarkets. Its unique pearl, made from real fruit juice and infused tea, contains maximum flavour. No need to go out and wait anymore: you can now get your favourite drink at any time of the day!

HOW MUCH SUGAR IS IN BOBBA BUBBLE TEA?

Each bottle of Bobba bubble tea contains less than 15 grams of sugar – compared to 100 grams of sugar for an in-store beverage. Another good reason to love Bobba!

WHAT ARE BOBBA'S INGREDIENTS AND NUTRITIONAL VALUES?

Bobba bubble tea is made with an infusion of real tea and unique fruit juice pearls. Each bottle contains less than 15 grams of sugar, making it a delicious and healthier alternative to traditional bubble tea.

DOES BOBBA BUBBLE TEA CONTAIN CAFFEINE?

Since Bobba bubble tea is made from an infusion of matcha tea, black tea or white tea, there is indeed a minimum level of caffeine.Matcha-infused flavours contain around 20-30 mg per bottle, which is 3 times less than coffee.
